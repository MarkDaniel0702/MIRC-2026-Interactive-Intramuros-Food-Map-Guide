      About this map

      What counts as Intramuros

      Every spot on this map sits inside the official administrative boundary of Intramuros — OpenStreetMap relation 103707, the same polygon drawn on the map as the lit ground.

      This is checked, not claimed. The list was built by querying only within that polygon, and a script re-tests all coordinates against it: node tools/verify-in-intramuros.mjs. Nothing from Binondo, Ermita, Malate or Quiapo appears here, because nothing outside the boundary can pass that check.

      What is on the map

      Two sets, switched with the Eat / See tabs: places to eat, and heritage sights with entrance fees and realistic visit times. Filters, search and the list all follow whichever tab is active.

      How price ranges work

      Food prices are shown as a tier plus an explicit peso band, using one scale across every listing:

      These are indicative estimates for a typical meal per person, not quoted prices. They come from published prices where those exist, standard chain pricing, and establishment type otherwise. Menus change — treat the band as a guide and confirm with the venue.

      Entrance fees and visit times

      Sight entrance fees come from the Intramuros Administration (intramuros.gov.ph) and the operators' own pages, checked . Unlike restaurant prices these are published and reasonably stable, but confirm before you travel. Discounted rates generally apply to students, seniors and PWD — bring ID.

      Visit durations are estimates for an unhurried look, not a rush. Walking times are straight-line distances from at 80 m per minute, so allow a little more on the ground.
